# 💼 ZeroAuth Commercial License & Acquisition Policy

ZeroAuth provides a **Dual-Licensing model** to support open-source indie hackers while legally and commercially protecting the intellectual property of the author (**MRJonam91**).

---

## 1. When do you need a Commercial License?

Under the **GNU AGPLv3**, you are legally required to provide the complete, open source code of any service or application that interacts with ZeroAuth over a network.

You **must** acquire a **ZeroAuth Commercial License** if you want to:
- Embed ZeroAuth into proprietary, closed-source SaaS products or enterprise applications.
- Keep your application's source code private without releasing it under AGPLv3.
- Remove or customize attribution / branding (White-label).
- Distribute closed commercial derivatives.
